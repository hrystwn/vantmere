VANTMÈRE
 

Three fabrics. Five silhouettes. No seasons.

Most labels plan around four collections a year, whether or not there's anything worth saying that season. We didn't want that constraint, so we removed it.
VANTMÈRE works from three fabrics and five silhouettes. Nothing gets added to hit a calendar date. Nothing gets discounted to clear space for the next drop, because there isn't a next drop on a schedule. There's only the next piece, when it's ready.
Everything is cut in small runs, numbered, and never restocked. When a run sells out, it's gone. That's not a scarcity tactic. It's just the honest result of not overproducing.
— VANTMÈRE
