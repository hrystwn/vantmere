VANTMÈRE
 

Black holds. Gray endures. White answers.

The palette is deliberately narrow: ink, paper, and three grays in between. No seasonal colorways, no limited-edition prints.
The Sculpted Overcoat is the clearest example of why. Double-faced Italian wool, cut long, with a collar built to stand on its own without interfacing tricks. It's not designed to look current. It's designed to still look right in a decade, which is a different design problem than most outerwear is solving for.
That's the same logic behind every piece in the collection. Long-term correctness over short-term newness.
— VANTMÈRE
