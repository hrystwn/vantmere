## How the sitemap works

The sitemap is **dynamic**, not a static file. `app/sitemap.ts` builds it at
request/build time from `lib/data/products.ts`, so adding or removing a product
automatically updates the sitemap — no manual edits needed.

- Sitemap URL: `https://vantmere.vercel.app/sitemap.xml`
- Referenced in robots: `app/robots.ts` → `https://vantmere.vercel.app/robots.txt`

## Design decisions

- **No `<priority>` / `<changefreq>`** — ignored by Google; omitted by design.
- **No `<lastmod>`** — the product data has no `updatedAt` field, so any date
  would be fabricated and identical across all URLs (worse than none; Google
  distrusts inaccurate lastmod). If you later add `updatedAt` to `Product`,
  wire it into `app/sitemap.ts` as `lastModified`.
- **Well under limits** — 17 URLs vs the 50,000-per-file protocol limit; no
  sitemap index needed.
- **All URLs canonical and indexable** — one canonical host
  (`vantmere.vercel.app`), HTTPS only, no redirects or noindexed pages included.

## After deploying

1. Verify `https://vantmere.vercel.app/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt` render.
2. Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console (Indexing → Sitemaps).
3. If you attach a custom domain later, update `BASE_URL` in `app/sitemap.ts`,
   the URL in `app/robots.ts`, and `metadataBase` in `app/layout.tsx` together.
