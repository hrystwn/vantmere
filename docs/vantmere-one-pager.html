VANTMÈRE

Quiet permanence.

VANTMÈRE is a luxury streetwear label built on restraint: garments made the way cities build monuments — slowly, in stone tones, meant to be walked past for decades. The brand pairs an editorial-fashion sensibility with a severe monochrome identity and a digital flagship designed to award-site standards.

Identity System

  • Voice — restrained, declarative, slightly cold. No exclamation points, ever.
  • Type — Playfair Display for oversized editorial statements; Space Grotesk for all-caps micro-labels.
  • Motifs — oversized serif numerals, self-drawing rules, film grain at 3%, a cursor that says VIEW and DRAG.

The Collection

  • Twelve pieces across four categories: Outerwear, Knitwear, Trousers, Accessories.
  • Priced $150–$1,480; anchored by the Sculpted Overcoat in double-faced Italian wool.
  • Cut in small runs, numbered, never restocked. Three fabrics, five silhouettes, no seasons.
  • Every product carries a two-sentence fabric story in the house voice.

Digital Flagship

  • Six-page marketing site: cinematic WebGL hero, pinned manifesto, editorial collection grid, horizontal-scroll lookbook.
  • Next.js 16 · GSAP + Lenis · React Three Fiber; fully responsive with reduced-motion degradation throughout.
  • All shopping UI is presentational — the store front opens with a "coming soon" drawer.
